Chemin de Galatry 3 · Corseaux VD

Renovation project

Two sketches built on the same model as the existing plans, both with the double garage at the top of the house, on the hillside, and an access road passing on the right. Variant A: a garage set into the slope with its own roof; you turn left to go in. Variant B: the garage is dug into the ground, straight in line with the road; you drive straight in going uphill, with no turn, and it is backfilled over, so there is no new roof. Both add a pool on the lake side and turn the house upside down: living room and kitchen move down to the pool level, bedrooms stay upstairs.

Lower floor

The lower floor, level with the pool, becomes the living floor, the same in both variants. The hall, laundry and old garage open into one large room (48 m²): you come down the stair straight into the living room, with the kitchen at the back against the hill and a large glass wall onto the pool. To the west, the old study becomes a spa room with a sauna (window onto the garden), and the two bedrooms stay, with their shower room; in front of them a terrace carries the pool deck on. The laundry moves into the old cellar, the boiler room takes a heat pump (the CECB recommends leaving gas) and the cistern becomes the pool plant room. The walls between hall, laundry and garage go: any load-bearing ones need a beam, for the engineer to confirm. Areas are measured on the redrawn plans, walls included: allow ±5 %.

Ground floor

Level ±0.00 · entrance · before and after · north up

The entrance floor keeps only the bedrooms. The living and dining rooms become two bedrooms (a new partition between them); the dining room, glazed onto the terrace, makes the main bedroom. The old kitchen, which already has water, splits into a study by the entrance (north window) and the main bathroom, opening onto the main bedroom. The two west bedrooms, the bathroom and the WC stay. In all: four bedrooms upstairs, two downstairs.

How to read this

  • Low: simple build, local contractors, no ramp heating. Mid: usual Riviera standard. High: premium finish; every item at its maximum at once, so a ceiling rather than a likely outcome.
  • Quantities are measured on the sketches (walls, areas, excavation volumes on the swissALTI3D terrain); unit prices come from Swiss cost guides. These are orders of magnitude to compare A and B, not quotes.
  • Architect and engineer fees: 12 / 15 / 18% of works, plus 15 / 25 / 40k for surveyor, geotechnical survey and permit. Contingency: 10 / 15 / 20%.

Not included

  • Renovating the upper floors (kitchen, bathrooms, envelope): see the scenarios.
  • Subsidies (Programme Bâtiments for the heat pump) and tax deductions for energy work, which lower the net cost.
  • Furniture, garage fit-out, home automation, financing costs during the works.

Why B costs more

  • A buried garage needs waterproof reinforced concrete (raft, walls, a slab that carries the backfill) and drainage behind it: roughly 30–40% more per m² than a garage set into the slope.
  • More excavation (deeper garage), but fewer retaining walls than A.
  • In return: no new visible volume or roof, and you drive straight in.

To check

Road grades

  • A about 20.5 %, B about 21.2 %: both steeper than the usual 12–15 % for a private drive. Today’s ramp to the forecourt is already about 25 %.
  • The parcel only touches the road at its bottom corner, so this needs a heated ramp, or a study of a longer route.

Variant B · buried garage

  • The roof slab carries up to about 2 m of soil: a heavier structure, with careful waterproofing and drainage.
  • About 3.3 m of cut at the door, with walls on both sides of the end of the road; the entrance forecourt adds a wall of up to about 3 m on the hill side.
  • The north-east corner is about 1 m from the boundary: rules for underground structures near boundaries need checking with the commune.

Variant A · rules

  • The garage’s north-west corner enters the 6 m band along the boundaries (PEP art. 10): acceptable only as an underground structure, to confirm with the commune.
  • A parking space on the garage roof can’t be reached by car: it would need about 15 m more road.

What this is

  • Orientation sketches, not an architect’s design: no structure, no costing. The pool, walls and road need a permit (CAMAC). A surveyor’s measured survey is needed before going further.
