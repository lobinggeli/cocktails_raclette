Main Menu

Espresso Martini

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • 1 oz Coffee liqueur (Kahlúa or Mr. Black)
  • 1 oz Fresh espresso (cooled) or cold brew concentrate
  • 1/2 oz Simple syrup (optional, adjust to taste)

Instructions:

  1. Shake hard with ice
  2. Double strain into coupe or martini glass
  3. Garnish with 3 coffee beans
Brew espresso ahead and chill, or use quality cold brew

Amaretto Sour

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 oz Amaretto
  • 3/4 oz Bourbon (optional but recommended - makes it less sweet)
  • 1 oz Fresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 oz Simple syrup
  • 1/2 oz Egg white
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Dry shake without ice first (to emulsify egg white)
  2. Add ice and shake again
  3. Strain into rocks glass
  4. Garnish with lemon wheel and cherry

Mezcal Sour

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz Mezcal
  • 3/4 oz Fresh lime juice
  • 1/2 oz Fresh lemon juice (or use all lime)
  • 1/2 oz Simple syrup or agave nectar
  • 1/2 oz Egg white

Instructions:

  1. Dry shake without ice first (to emulsify egg white)
  2. Add ice and shake again
  3. Strain into rocks glass
  4. Optional: smoked salt rim, garnish with lime wheel

Negroni

Ingredients:

  • 1 oz Gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz Sweet vermouth

Instructions:

  1. Stir with ice for 30 seconds
  2. Strain into rocks glass over large ice cube
  3. Garnish with orange peel (express oils over drink)
Can batch this easily - just combine equal parts and stir before serving

Pisco Sour

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz Pisco
  • 1 oz Fresh lime juice
  • 3/4 oz Simple syrup
  • 1/2 oz Egg white
  • Angostura bitters (for garnish)

Instructions:

  1. Dry shake without ice first (to emulsify egg white)
  2. Add ice and shake hard
  3. Strain into coupe or rocks glass
  4. Add 3 drops of Angostura bitters on top of foam
  5. Draw a design through the drops with a toothpick (optional)

Instructions:

Old Fashioned

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz Bourbon or Rye

Instructions:

Penicillin

Ingredients:

  • 2 oz Blended Scotch
  • 3/4 oz Fresh lemon juice
  • 3/4 oz Honey-ginger syrup
  • 1/4 oz Islay Scotch (float on top)

Instructions:

  1. Shake and strain into rocks glass
Honey-ginger syrup: Equal parts honey and hot water, add fresh ginger slices, steep and strain

🇨🇭 Swiss Shopping Tips

Tequila Recommendations

What to Look For:

  • Always choose "100% Agave" on the label
  • Get Blanco (Silver) for cocktails - unaged, pure agave flavor
  • Don't waste money on Reposado/Añejo for mixed drinks
Best Choice in Switzerland:
Olmeca Altos Plata - Widely available at Coop, Manor, Denner. 100% agave, made for cocktails, CHF 35-45. This is what Swiss bartenders use.

Other Good Options:

  • El Jimador Blanco - Common in Swiss stores, 100% agave, CHF 30-40
  • Espolòn Blanco - Sometimes at Manor, excellent value, CHF 40-50
AVOID:
• Sierra Tequila - Very common in Switzerland but NOT 100% agave
• Jose Cuervo Gold - Unless it says "Tradicional", it's mixto
• Patrón - Overpriced (CHF 60-80+), not worth it for cocktails

Mezcal Recommendations

What to Look For:

  • "100% Agave" (or "100% Maguey")
  • Joven (young/unaged) - best for cocktails
  • Espadin agave variety - most common, good for mixing
Perfect Choice:
Montelobos Espadin - Excellent for cocktails. 100% Espadin agave, balanced smokiness (not overwhelming), perfect for Mezcal Sour. Made for mixing, bartenders love it. CHF 55-70 at Manor or Globalus.

Alternative Good Options:

  • Illegal Mezcal Joven - Available in Switzerland/Europe, smooth, CHF 55-65
  • Los Amantes Joven - Sometimes at specialty shops, CHF 60-70
  • Del Maguey Vida - Specifically made for cocktails, CHF 70-80

Where to Shop:

  • Manor - Best spirits selection
  • Globalus/Drinks of the World - Specialty stores, better mezcal selection
  • Coop - Good for tequila, limited mezcal
  • Denner - Budget-friendly, limited selection
