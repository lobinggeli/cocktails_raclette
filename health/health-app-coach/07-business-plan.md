### AI-Powered Daily Health Coach

*Version 1.0 — April 2026 | Confidential*

## Executive Summary

**Vital** is a daily AI health coach that synthesizes data from wearables (Apple Watch, Garmin, Oura Ring), activity trackers, and blood biomarker tests into one personalized coaching experience — delivered every morning in under 30 seconds.

The market is ready. European consumers already spend €1,000+ per year on Apple Watches, Oura Rings, and blood tests. No app today combines these into actionable daily guidance. Existing players (Superpower, InsideTracker, Levels) either focus on one data source, provide generic insights, or ignore the European market entirely.

**Vital's edge:** True multi-source integration + daily AI coaching that knows your context (last workout, last drink, last blood test) — built for the Swiss and German-speaking European market.

**Business model:** B2C subscription at €69/month, scaling to B2B corporate wellness contracts in Year 2.

**3-year targets:**
- Year 1: 2,000 paying users → €1.4M ARR
- Year 2: 12,000 paying users → €9.9M ARR
- Year 3: 40,000 paying users → €33.1M ARR

**Investment needed:** €800K seed to fund 18 months of product development and initial market launch.

## 1. The Problem

### People are drowning in health data with no guidance

The average health-conscious European in 2026:
- Wears a smartwatch that tracks HRV, sleep, and heart rate
- May use an Oura Ring for recovery scoring
- Tracks workouts on Strava or Garmin Connect
- Gets blood tests once or twice a year (cortisol, testosterone, ferritin, vitamins)
- Logs food in an app like Lifesum

**They have more health data than any human in history — and no idea what to do with it.**

Each app shows its own slice. Apple Health shows sleep. Strava shows pace. The blood test PDF sits in a drawer. No one connects the dots. No one says: "Your HRV has been down for 4 days, your cortisol was high in your last test, and you had drinks twice this week — here's what to do today."

That synthesis doesn't exist. **Vital is that synthesis.**

### The scale of the gap
- 45% of European adults own a fitness wearable (2025)
- Only 12% report acting on the data their wearable provides
- Average user switches health apps within 90 days due to lack of personalization
- Swiss consumers spend ~CHF 600-1,200/year on health tracking hardware + tests, with no software layer to unify it

---

## 2. The Solution

### Vital: One coach. One daily brief. Unlimited context.

Vital connects to every data source a user already has, then delivers:

1. **Daily morning brief** — a 30-second read that tells you what your body needs today, based on last night's sleep, your HRV trend, your recent workouts, and anything you've logged (stress, alcohol, travel)

2. **Always-on coach** — ask anything, log anything. Tap one button to tell the coach "had drinks last night" or "feeling off today." The coach remembers and adjusts.

3. **Blood result integration** — upload any lab PDF. Vital extracts your biomarkers and cross-references them with your wearable data. First app to connect "your cortisol is elevated" with "your HRV has been low for 10 days."

4. **Weekly synthesis** — what the data patterns revealed, what's improving, what to focus on next week.

**What makes Vital different from every competitor:**
- Combines ALL data sources (wearables + biomarkers + activity)
- Context-aware AI (knows about last night, not just metrics)
- Built for Europe (Swiss labs, GDPR-first, German language)
- Daily habit, not a dashboard you check monthly

## 8. Technology Overview

### Core Stack
- **iOS (Swift):** Native HealthKit integration; cannot be done via backend
- **Terra SDK:** Unified wearable API (Garmin, Oura, Strava) — eliminates 3 months of integration work
- **FastAPI (Python):** Async backend, handles concurrent polling for thousands of users
- **TimescaleDB:** PostgreSQL extension for time-series health metrics + relational data
- **Claude API (Anthropic):** Primary AI reasoning engine for coaching; 200K context window, prompt caching
- **Redis:** Session management, rate limiting, job queuing

### Key Technical Differentiators
- **Context-aware prompting:** Each coaching request includes 7-day metric trends + recent event log + biomarker snapshot → specific advice, not generic
- **PDF lab parsing:** Claude Vision extracts structured biomarker data from any lab PDF — zero lab partnership required at launch
- **AI cost efficiency:** ~€0.50/user/month in AI costs at €69/month revenue = <1% of revenue

### Data & Privacy
- All health data stored on EU/Swiss servers (AWS Frankfurt or Azure Switzerland)
- End-to-end encryption for biomarker data
- Full GDPR + Swiss nDSG compliance
- DPAs signed with all third-party processors (Terra, Anthropic, cloud provider)

## 10. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strava AI prohibition extends to other platforms | Medium | High | Terra SDK as abstraction layer; design AI coaching to work without Strava data |
| Apple restricts HealthKit API | Low | High | Build Swiss lab + Oura integration as fallback; don't single-source depend on Apple |
| Swissmedic classifies app as medical device | Low-Medium | High | Legal review before launch; strict coaching-not-diagnosis language; no treatment claims |
| User churn from poor AI coaching quality | High | High | Invest in prompt quality early; human coach review of AI outputs; rapid iteration |
| Competitor (Superpower/WHOOP) enters Europe | Medium | Medium | Speed to market; local lab partnerships as moat; community brand |
| Data breach | Low | Very High | Encryption at rest + transit; regular audits; breach response plan; cyber insurance |
| GDPR enforcement action | Low | High | Swiss data protection lawyer from day 1; DPAs with all processors |
