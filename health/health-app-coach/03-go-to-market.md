# Health Coach App — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target Segments (in priority order)

### 1. Biohackers & Quantified Self Enthusiasts (Launch)
- **Market:** Europe biohacking market €7.46B (2025), 17.4% CAGR
- **Profile:** Already own Oura Ring, Apple Watch, get blood tests, use Strava. Comfortable paying €50-150/month for premium insights.
- **Why first:** Understand data aggregation, expect AI-driven insights, low acquisition friction via existing communities (Quantified Self meetups, biohacking forums)
- **Risk:** Small initial TAM (~50K-100K in DACH + Switzerland), but high engagement and retention

### 2. Preventive Health / Health-Conscious Professionals (Scale)
- Ages 30-55, above-average income, motivated by avoiding disease
- Less tech-savvy than biohackers but higher willingness to pay
- Can be reached via corporate wellness programs

### 3. Corporate Wellness (Phase 2 wedge, not Phase 1)
- Don't start by selling to HR — build B2C first, then pull companies in
- B2B sales cycles are 6-12 months; don't let this distract from B2C traction

## Pricing

**Positioning:** The user already pays €400 for Apple Watch, €300 for Oura, €200-400 for blood tests. We are the intelligence layer on top — the synthesis they're not getting.

| Tier | Price | Included |
|---|---|---|
| Free | €0 | Wearable aggregation, basic dashboard |
| Core | €69/month (or €59/month annual) | Daily AI coaching, trend analysis, lab PDF upload |
| Premium | €120/month | Quarterly blood tests subsidized + monthly human coach check-in |

**Competitor benchmarks:**
- Superpower: $199/year (biomarker snapshot, basic app)
- Levels: $499-1,999/year (CGM focus)
- InsideTracker: $600-700 initial test + $340 retests
- WHOOP: ~$30/month (device subscription)

**Churn risk:** Health subscription apps see 5-10% monthly churn. Retention levers: personalization depth, wearable integration stickiness, weekly summaries that show progress.

## Acquisition Channels

### Tier 1 — Low CAC, High Intent
| Channel | CAC | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| App Store Optimization (ASO) | €20-50 | Health app searches are high-intent. Keywords: "AI health coach," "wearable insights," "biomarker tracking" |
| Community & referral | €25-65 | Quantified Self meetups, r/biohacking, r/quantifiedself, Strava groups. Referred users have 30-50% lower churn. |
| SEO content | €30-80 (delayed) | "How to decode your Oura data," "HRV guide," "biomarker interpretation." Start 3 months before launch. |

### Tier 2 — Broader Reach
| Channel | CAC | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Influencer partnerships | €40-130 | Biohacking/health creators, affiliate model (€5-10/user). Avoid generic fitness influencers. |
| TikTok/Instagram | €50-150 | Short-form wellness content. Builds awareness for 25-40 age group. |

### Avoid Initially
- Google Ads health keywords: €80-250 CAC, high regulatory scrutiny
- Facebook paid: €60-180 CAC, lower intent

**Launch plan:**
1. Seed 500 biohacker users via community channels (budget: ~€5-10K)
2. ASO from day 1
3. SEO content starts 3 months before launch
4. 3-5 influencer partnerships at launch (€3-5K each, affiliate model)

**Target CAC:** €40-60 for initial cohort

## B2B Corporate Wellness (Phase 2)

**Market:** Europe corporate wellness €19.14B (2024) → €38.02B (2034)

**The B2C → B2B2C path:**
- **Months 1-12:** Build B2C base of 2,000-5,000 engaged users
- **Months 12-18:** Approach SME employers (50-500 employees) with case studies and outcome data
- **Corporate pricing:** €5-8/employee/month (or €3,000-5,000/month for a 500-person company)
- **Employee co-pay:** Employee pays €25-40/month, employer subsidizes the rest

**Why B2B works:**
- Employer handles internal promotion — reduces your CAC to near zero for that cohort
- Higher LTV: 1-3 year corporate contracts
- Compliance builds once (GDPR/nDSG), sell many times

**Insurance channel (longer term):**
Some Swiss insurers (Helsana, CSS, Migros Krankenkasse) cover "digital health coaching" under wellness budgets. Worth exploring after launch — don't depend on it.

## 3-Phase GTM Roadmap

### Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Validation
- Seed 500 biohacker/quantified self users via community channels
- Test pricing at €49, €69, €99/month
- Secure DPAs with Apple, Garmin, Strava (GDPR compliance)
- Engage Swiss data protection lawyer

### Phase 2 (Months 4-8): B2C Expansion
- Scale ASO + organic SEO to 5K-10K users
- Launch referral program (€10 credit per referred friend)
- Publish 20-30 SEO articles on biomarker interpretation, preventive health
- Run 3-5 influencer partnerships
- Target CAC: €40-60; aim for 3:1 LTV:CAC ratio

### Phase 3 (Months 9-12): B2B Pilot + Expansion
- Approach 10 SME employers (50-500 employees) with pilot offers
- Negotiate corporate pricing
- Add admin/team features for corporate deployment
- Expand into Germany, Austria (German market is 3-4x Switzerland TAM)
- Explore insurance white-label partnerships
