When to Talk to Your Pediatrician

  • No social smile by 2 months
  • Does not follow objects with eyes by 3 months
  • Does not babble or imitate sounds by 6 months
  • Does not sit independently by 9 months
  • Does not use gestures (waving, pointing) by 12 months
  • Loss of previously acquired skills at any age
  • Does not respond to their name by 9 months
  • No single words by 16 months
  • Persistent lack of eye contact
  • Extreme difficulty with feeding or sleep that does not improve

Sources: CDC Milestones · American Academy of Pediatrics · WHO Guidelines · HealthyChildren.org
